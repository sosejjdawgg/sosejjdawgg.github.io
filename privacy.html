Privacy Policy

Socks! (Alpha) · Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy explains how the game Socks! ("the Game") handles information. The Game is made by James Scott ("we", "us"), who is responsible for it under UK data protection law (UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018). Contact: jamscoot22@gmail.com

The short version

We run no servers and do not sell personal information. The only personal information we receive is the email address you choose to send if you ask to join the closed beta (see below). The Game has no accounts of its own and no analytics. The Android app shows ads through Google AdMob, sells an optional "Remove ads" purchase through Google Play, and offers optional Daily Pile leaderboards through Google Play Games. Those Google services handle some data, explained below. The web version has none of them.

What is stored, and where

To work, the Game saves a few things on your own device only:

  • high scores and the names you type for the score boards;
  • your Daily Pile result for the day and your practice best;
  • the sock you design in The Daily Pile and your Daily Pile stats (top score, pile wins);
  • your coins and the Sock Shop items you have unlocked;
  • that you accepted the Terms, so you are not asked every time.

This information stays on your device and we cannot see it. When you are not signed in to Google Play Games (and always in the web version), the Daily Pile rankings are sample scores built into the Game, compared with your score on your device.

Google Play Games (Android app, optional)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games on your Android device, finishing your official Daily Pile run sends these to Google Play Games Services so it can show the daily leaderboards:

  • your Daily Pile score for the day;
  • a short code describing your sock design (colours, pattern, hat and accessory) and your Daily Pile stats (top score and number of pile wins).

Other players see your Play Games profile name, your score and your sock on the leaderboards. Your Play Games profile and sign-in are run by Google under Google's own Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy); we do not receive your Google account details. You can play everything without signing in, sign out at any time in the Play Games settings, and manage or delete your Play Games profile and its data there. Children with Google accounts supervised through Family Link need a parent's approval to create a Play Games profile.

The web version has no online leaderboards.

Ads (Android app)

The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob: an optional ad you can choose to watch to keep playing, and occasionally a full-screen ad between classic games. There are no ads in The Daily Pile.

To show and measure ads, Google may use your device's advertising ID, your approximate location (from your IP address), information about your device and how you interact with ads. In the UK and EEA, the app shows Google's consent form the first time you play, so you can choose whether ads are personalised. You can change that choice at any time with AD CHOICES on the main menu, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. With personalised ads turned off, Google still shows ads but does not personalise them, and uses data only as needed to serve them and prevent fraud. Google's use of this data is covered by Google's Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy) and "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

The "Remove ads" purchase

If you buy "Remove ads", Google Play handles the payment and your payment details. We never see your card or bank details. The app only checks with Google Play whether you own the purchase, so it can turn off the full-screen ads, including after a reinstall.

Backups

On Android, your phone's own backup service (for example Google's Android backup) may include the Game's saved scores in your device backup, if you have backups turned on. That is controlled by you and your device provider, not by us.

Playing in a web browser

If you play the web version, the website hosting it (for example itch.io) may automatically log technical information such as your IP address and browser type, as all websites do. That is covered by the host's own privacy policy. The Game itself sends nothing to us.

Beta sign-ups

If you ask to join the Socks! closed beta from our website, the website opens your own email app with a message to jamscoot22@gmail.com containing the Google account email you typed. Nothing is sent until you press Send, and the website itself stores nothing. We use your email address only to add you to the Google Play test and to tell you when you've been added (our lawful basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by emailing us). We keep it until the game launches publicly, or less if you ask, then delete it. The test itself runs on Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy.

Children

The Android app is intended for players aged 13 and over. The web version is suitable for all ages and collects nothing. We ask players not to type their real full name, or anyone else's, into the score boards.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or clear the site data for the Game in your browser. This removes everything the Game has saved.

Your rights

You have rights under UK data protection law, including to ask what information we hold about you. As we hold none, there is nothing for us to give or delete, but you can contact us at jamscoot22@gmail.com with any question. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes

If a future version adds other online features, we will update this policy before that version is released, and the Game will ask you to review it.
