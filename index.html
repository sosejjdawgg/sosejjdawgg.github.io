WHAT'S INSIDE

Campaign ★★★★★

Five levels, five stars each. Reach the sock limit and take your stars, or keep going for glory. Freeze, Magnet, Pair Pulse and Soft Lock pickups.

The Daily Pile

The same pile for everyone, every day. Practise all you like, then take your one shot. Beat your mates on the UK and world boards.

Sock Shop

Earn coins by playing. Dress your sock in colours, patterns, hats and extras. Some gear can only be earned in the campaign.

Quick Game

Endless sorting with a new twist every level: swaps, spring drawers, golden socks, stripes and double drops.
